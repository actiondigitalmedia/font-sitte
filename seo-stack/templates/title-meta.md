# Title & meta patterns

## Font entity

| Field | Pattern |
|-------|---------|
| Title (≤60) | `{{name}}: Free {{category}} Font ({{license_short}})` |
| Title alt | `Download {{name}} Font Free — {{license_short}}, Commercial Use` |
| Meta (≤155) | `Download {{name}} free. {{category}} typeface with {{variant_count}} styles. {{license_short}} license — commercial use {{commercial_use}}. Official {{source}} link.` |

## Category hub

| Field | Pattern |
|-------|---------|

## Commercial-use hub

| Field | Pattern |
|-------|---------|
| Title | `Free Fonts for Commercial Use (OFL & Open Source)` |
| Meta | `Stop risking personal-use fonts. Explore commercial-safe libre fonts — OFL, Apache, and more — with clear licenses and live previews.` |

## CTR repair tips

- Put the matching query language early (`commercial use`, `download`, font name)
- Keep brand short or at end until brand is known
- Prefer specifics (OFL, N styles) over adjectives ("amazing", "beautiful")
